Homelab Family AI

Homelab Family AI is a privately hosted AI assistant designed for personal household use.

Features

The assistant helps authorized household members access shared family calendar information and answer general questions.

Google Calendar Integration

Homelab Family AI uses the Google Calendar API with read-only permissions to retrieve upcoming events from an authorized family calendar.

The application does not create, modify, or delete Google Calendar events.

Access

The assistant is privately hosted and is not available for public registration.
