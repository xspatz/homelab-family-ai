Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Purpose

Homelab Family AI is a privately hosted assistant intended for personal household use.

Google Calendar Data

The application requests read-only access to Google Calendar events to answer questions about upcoming family activities and schedules.

Data Processing

Calendar information is retrieved through a privately hosted API and may be processed by a locally hosted AI model to generate responses.

Data Storage

Google OAuth credentials are stored on the private application server.

Calendar information may appear in assistant conversation history or application logs, depending on the system configuration.

Data Sharing

Calendar information is intended for authorized household members and is not sold to advertisers.

Access and Revocation

Google Calendar authorization can be revoked through Google Account security settings.
